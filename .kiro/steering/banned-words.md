# Banned Words — Content & Copy Rules

Never use the following words in any user-facing text, UI copy, translations, descriptions, or generated content — in either English or Khmer:

## English
- **exam** (use "test", "quiz", "challenge", or "assessment" instead)
- **driving** (use "road safety", "traffic", or "travel" instead)

## Khmer
- **វេទិកា** (use "គេហទំព័រ" instead)
- **ហ្វឹកហ្វឺន** (use "សំរាប់រៀន" or "សំណួរ" instead)
- **ប្រឡង** (use "ប្រកួត" or "តេស្ត" instead)
- **បើកបរ** (use "សុវត្ថិភាពចរាចរណ៍" or "ចរាចរណ៍" instead)
- **ល្បែង** (use "សកម្មភាព" instead)
- **វីរបុរស** (use "បញ្ញាបរ" instead)
- **ផ្សងព្រែង** (use "ដំណើរ" instead)

## Why

This project is a road safety education platform for students of all ages. It is NOT a "driving school" app and NOT an "exam preparation" tool. The framing should always focus on **learning**, **road safety knowledge**, and **confidence through understanding**.

## Exceptions

- Internal DB slugs/IDs (e.g. `slug: "driving"` in category data) are allowed since they are not user-visible.
- The word "test" (as in quiz/test) is acceptable.
- Technical documentation or algorithm docs are exempt.
