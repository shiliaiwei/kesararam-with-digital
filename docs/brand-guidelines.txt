01 · BRAND STORY / ដំណើររឿងម៉ាក
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KH: ផ្លូវនៅកម្ពុជាពោរពេញដោយផ្លាកសញ្ញា និងច្បាប់ ប៉ុន្តែមនុស្សជាច្រើន
    មិនទាន់យល់ច្បាស់។ វិថីសុវត្ថិភាព បង្វែរច្បាប់ចរាចរណ៍ឲ្យទៅជាមេរៀន
    ល្បែង និងមតិត្រឡប់ភ្លាមៗ ដើម្បីឲ្យយុវជន និងប្រជាពលរដ្ឋ រៀនបានងាយ
    ហើយធ្វើដំណើរដោយសុវត្ថិភាពនៅជីវិតពិត។
EN: Cambodia's roads are full of signs and rules — but too few people
    truly understand them. The Way of Safety turns traffic law into
    lessons, games, and instant feedback, so youth and everyday riders
    learn easily and stay safe in real life.

02 · MISSION & VISION / បេសកកម្ម និងចក្ខុវិស័យ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION (KH): ធ្វើឲ្យការរៀនសុវត្ថិភាពចរាចរណ៍ ងាយ ទំនើប និងសប្បាយ
              សម្រាប់យុវជនកម្ពុជា។
MISSION (EN): Make traffic-safety learning easy, modern, and fun for
              Cambodia's youth.
VISION  (KH): សហគមន៍កម្ពុជាដែលមនុស្សគ្រប់គ្នាស្គាល់ច្បាប់ និងធ្វើដំណើរ
              ដោយសុវត្ថិភាព។
VISION  (EN): A Cambodia where everyone knows the rules and travels safely.

03 · BRAND VALUES / តម្លៃម៉ាក
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ទំនើប                      Modern        — feels like an app, not a textbook
  ត្រឹមត្រូវ                  Accurate      — real Cambodian road law, never guesses
  ងាយយល់                     Easy          — plain Khmer, a teen gets it instantly
  យុវជន                      Youth         — energy, color, play; made FOR youth
  សុវត្ថិភាពសម្រាប់ជីវិតពិត      Real-life safe — knowledge that protects you tomorrow
  បង្កើតសុវត្ថិភាពសម្រាប់សហគមន៍  Community     — building a safer society together

04 · AUDIENCE / អ្នកប្រើប្រាស់
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Primary   : Youth & students (Khmer-first)
  Secondary : General public / everyday riders in Cambodia

05 · TAGLINE SYSTEM / ពាក្យស្លោក
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Official / clean : រៀនច្បាប់ចរាចរណ៍ ស្គាល់ផ្លាកសញ្ញា ធ្វើដំណើរដោយសុវត្ថិភាព
  Emotional / hero : ចំណេះដឹងថ្ងៃនេះ ការពារជីវិតថ្ងៃស្អែក
  Short / social   : ស្គាល់ផ្លាក · ដឹងច្បាប់ · ទៅដោយសុវត្ថិភាព

06 · BRAND TONE & VOICE / សំឡេងម៉ាក
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE LINE (KH): ច្បាស់លាស់ ងាយយល់ និងពោរពេញដោយថាមពលវ័យក្មេង —
               ដូចមិត្តម្នាក់ដែលចេះច្បាប់ ហើយចង់ឲ្យអ្នកមានសុវត្ថិភាព។
ONE LINE (EN): Clear, easy, and full of youthful energy — like a friend
               who knows the rules and wants you safe.

  WE SOUND LIKE (✔)              WE NEVER SOUND LIKE (✘)
  - warm and direct              - stiff legal / government Khmer
  - short everyday Khmer          - scary or guilt-tripping
  - encouraging, celebrates wins  - childish / joking about real danger
  - confident on facts            - preachy or talking down

  TONE BY CONTEXT:
    Games        -> playful
    Lessons      -> calm & clear
    Leaderboard  -> proud
    Safety warn  -> serious but kind

07 · WRITING RULES / ច្បាប់សរសេរ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Plain, everyday Khmer. Short sentences.
  - No stiff / legal / government-document Khmer.
  LOCKED Q&A RULE — never use inside any Q&A content:
    emoji   ·   & (ampersand)   ·   * (asterisk)   ·   stiff/legal Khmer

PART C — HOW WE LOOK / របៀបយើងមើលទៅ
================================================================================

08 · LOGO / ឡូហ្គោ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Primary: brand name វិថីសុវត្ថិភាព + sign mark
  - Lockups: Khmer-only, Khmer+EN, mark-only
  - Keep clear space around logo (= height of one letter)
  DON'T: stretch · recolor · add shadow/gradient · rotate · crowd it

09 · COLOR PALETTE / ពណ៌  (traffic-sign system)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Caution Yellow   primary accent, energy     attention / youth / road
  Ink Black        text, borders, structure   authority / clarity
  Clean White      background, space          modern / readable
  Stop Red         warnings, errors           danger / prohibition
  Go Green         success, correct           safe / progress
  Info Blue        tips, guidance             information
  (HEX values pulled from the live kwd-brand-design palette when designed.)

10 · TYPOGRAPHY / ពុម្ពអក្សរ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FONT: Google Sans — used for ALL text, including Khmer.
  RULE: Never use Kantumruy Pro. One family, different weights.
  Scale: H1 Bold (big) -> H2 -> Body Regular -> Caption -> Numbers Bold

11 · AESTHETIC & GRAPHIC ELEMENTS / រចនាបថ
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Style: Brutalist + caution-tape + road-sign — bold, high-contrast, road.
  Signatures:
    - thick black borders
    - caution-tape stripes
    - real road-sign shapes (octagon, triangle, circle)
    - big bold numbers
    - flat solid colors (no soft gradients)
