### Business Model
**4-Tier Subscription Structure:**
1. **FREE** - $0/month
   - 10 players/session, 1 session/month, 1 category access
   
2. **TEACHER PRO** - $10/month
   - 50 players, unlimited sessions, all 8 categories
   - Analytics, advanced game modes
   
3. **SCHOOL** - $39/month
   - 100 players, 5 teacher accounts
   - Class management, full analytics
   
4. **INSTITUTE** - $49/month (Best Value)
   - 200 players, 10 teacher accounts
   - Priority support, API access, white-label options
