Pricing & Subscription Theory
Target Users (Who to Pick)
Think in 3 tiers:

Students (free, growth engine) — they join games, learn signs, compete. They don't pay but they drive adoption. Every student who uses it tells their teacher.

Teachers / Instructors (core paying users) — they host live sessions, track progress, need more questions and analytics. This is your primary revenue target.

Driving Schools / Institutions (high-value B2B) — they want multiple teacher accounts, branded experience, bulk student management. Highest revenue per customer.

Available Plans
Free

Join live games as student
Browse traffic signs (limited categories)
Basic quiz practice (limited questions/day)
No account required to play
Teacher Pro (~$9–12/month)

Unlimited live game sessions
All question categories unlocked
Session analytics & student performance reports
Custom session settings (difficulty, time, scoring)
Up to 50 students per session
School / Institution (~$49–99/month)

Everything in Teacher Pro
Up to 10 teacher accounts
Up to 200 students per session
Admin dashboard with class management
Priority support + custom branding option
What to Lock Behind Subscription
The key is: free users must feel the value, but hit a wall that makes paying obvious.

Lock these:

Hosting live sessions (free = 1 session/month, 10 players max)
Full question bank (free = 1 category only)
Analytics & leaderboard history
Export results / reports
Advanced game modes (marathon, team battle)
Removing the "Powered by KWD" branding
Keep free:

Joining as a student (always free — this is your viral loop)
Basic sign browsing
A few practice questions per day
Revenue Logic
The viral loop is: student plays → tells teacher → teacher upgrades. Lock the teacher tools, not the student experience. Schools pay for the admin layer. Keep the student side frictionless forever.
